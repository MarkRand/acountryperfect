**The Greater Wandin Valley
Mapping Expedition**
 
 

---

**One thing that I've always
wanted to do is make a map of where everything
(and everyone) is in Wandin Valley. We hear road
names and the amount of time it takes to get from
point A to point B but really we have little else
to go on.**

**What I'd like to do, with
everyone's help, is cobble together this rough
map based on things the characters say and things
we observe on the show (e.g. we know that Anna
and Tom live across the road from Kate and Harry
from events on the show). Once I have some
"concrete" information, I'll create
(either digitally or manually) maps of the
various areas, with an eye to ultimately creating
one huge tell-all map.**

**In the
meantime, what I'll do is post here the facts
that I know and any that others pass on to me
(the initial "database" will be
uploaded over the next several weeks). If you
want to help out, please**
**...all help is very
welcome. The point is that I will probably do
this anyway, regardless of whether or not anyone
else helps out, but it'll go much faster if at
the very least people can drop me a line whenever
they spot a geographical reference on the show.**
