## Filming Locations in New South Wales

**Filming Locations**

**Most
of the outdoors scenes of Wandin Valley were
actually filmed in and around the Windsor area
towns of Pitt Town and Ebenezer. Indoor scenes
were filmed at Channel Seven's studios in Epping,
outside of Sydney.**

**Other
"location" scenes were shot on the
cruise ship the Oriana, in Sydney and Perth, and
at nearby racetracks.**
