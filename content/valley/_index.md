## Welcome to Wandin Valley

Set in the picturesque rolling hills of rural New South Wales (situated fictional kilometres between Burrigan and Magnolia Vale), **Wandin Valley** was the beloved home of *A Country Practice* for over a decade.

Explore the town, the hospital, and real-world filming sites:

- [**Town Map & Sites**](./town-map/): A tour of Wandin Valley's streets, shops, public hall, police station, and properties.
- [**Hospital Architecture & Layout**](./hospital-layout/): Floor plans and room-by-room architectural tour of the Wandin Valley Bush Nursing Hospital.
- [**Real-World Filming Locations**](./filming-locations/): The historic towns of Pitt Town, Oakville, and Windsor in the Hawkesbury district where the show was filmed.
- [**Frank & Shirley Gilroy Memorial Rose Garden**](./rose-garden/): A dedicated tribute to one of Australian TV's most iconic couples.
