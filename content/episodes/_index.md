## Complete A Country Practice Episode Guide

*A Country Practice* originally ran for **1,058 episodes** across 13 seasons on the Seven Network from November 1981 to November 1993. It was produced by JNP Productions and filmed in Pitt Town, New South Wales and the ATN-7 studios in Epping, Sydney.

Episodes were broadcast as two one-hour instalments per week, telling complete stories across each pair.
