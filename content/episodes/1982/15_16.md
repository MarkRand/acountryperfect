### Synopsis

Dr. Elliott delays returning to the hospital when he meets a lady trucker; a boy's punishment creates havoc for some of the hospital staff. Bob Hatfield works on the Jones' new indoor loo. The arrival of Dr. Elliott's new friend sets off shock waves at Wandin Valley; Bob Hatfield's dentist disappears.
