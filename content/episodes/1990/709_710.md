### Synopsis

King" Former pop idol Martin Glass injures himself riding on Cris' new motorcycle. Luke tries to help song-writer Anna Collins overcome her disability. Cathy apprehends Ivy Clements in the National Park when she is caught stealing some rare orchids. Cookie is distraught when his young niece, Sam, goes missing. Anna Collins takes action when she discovers Martin Glass has been claiming her songs as his own and is about to ditch her for another keyboard player. A mystery patient confuses everyone at the hospital.
