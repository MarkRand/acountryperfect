### Synopsis

Frank and Sgt Bruce Sharp pursue young Danny Lalor in a high-speed car chase. Shirley gets a lesson in politics. Frank is blamed for the death of Danny Lalor by the locals but only he knows the truth but he is reluctant to shift the blame onto Bruce Sharp. Luke and Steve find a twenty-year-old time capsule in Ann Brennan's garden and they decide to plant one of their own. Shirley is enjoying the world of cut-throat politics. Frank is cleared of any blame by the authorities but has trouble forgiving himself for his part in Danny's death.
