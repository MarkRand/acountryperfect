### Synopsis

Corina Kennedy asks Terence for an abortion. Ann Brennan seems to be the only one unaware of Wandin Valley's betting ring. Cris and Tim Youngblood almost come to blows after a disagreement over Ninja Turtle mania. Jessie insists on Cris buying her one because everyone at her school has one and when he puts his foot down Jessie swaps her expensive bike with Tim's young son. Luke receives confirmation of a job in Sydney. Does he intend to leave the Valley for good?
