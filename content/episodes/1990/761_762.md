### Synopsis

The arrival of shearer's cook Gladys Carter sends Cookie running. Her son Cholmondely, meanwhile, is smitten by Ann. Luke is suspicious after Ted Campbell appeals to Steve for help. Matt and Lucy's wedding plans change yet again. Jessie and her friend Bianca Jones become ill after playing tunnel ball in the park. Luke enters Steve in the Wool Queen contest. Cookie believes he has found a long lost son in Cholmondely after he recalls a brief affair with Gladys many years ago. A reluctant Steve is crowned Wool Queen.
