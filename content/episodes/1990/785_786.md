### Synopsis

Cris Kouros' parents, Theo and Claire, arrive in Wandin Valley to see their son and grand-daughter Jessie. Matt discovers that marriage to Lucy is not all peace and harmony. Claire Kouros tells a distraught Cns that she is dying from cancer and doesn't have very long to live. Frank has problems casting for the musical. Claire Kouros dies in Theo's arms at Camelot. Esme is in despair over Theo, who is grieving over his dead wife, but is giving everyone at Camelot a hard time.
