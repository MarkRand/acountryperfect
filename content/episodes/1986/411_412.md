### Synopsis

Shirley organises a petition against the impending army base in Wandin Valley. Peter is very concerned about one of his pupils after he smashes up the classroom for no apparent reason. Peter's students are angry that the elders of the Valley haven't asked their opinion on the army base as it is their future. The Prime Minister, Bob Hawke, makes a special appearance at a rock concert to show his support to the kids of Burrigan High on their stand against nuclear bases in Wandin Valley.
