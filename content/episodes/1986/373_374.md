### Synopsis

Ben thinks he's a failure when the farmers of the district fall asleep during his speech. Brendan gets caught in a storm while he is out on a fishing trip with the hospital patients and school children. Cookie has a horrific accident and the doctors battle to save his life. Judy is worried when she has a strange phone call and hears noises outside her flat. Everything that could go wrong does go wrong for Brendan on his fishing trip.
