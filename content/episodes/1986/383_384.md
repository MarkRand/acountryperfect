### Synopsis

Judy is put in an embarrassing situation when she forgets the combination of the hospital safe. Dr. Wood and Kell find a young boy on the side of the road who's been hit by a car. Brendan decides to go to Albury to see Sarah. Kelly's negative article on Dr. Wood causes him to resign and leaves Kelly very unpopular with the locals. Brendan and Sarah return to the Valley with some good news for their friends.
