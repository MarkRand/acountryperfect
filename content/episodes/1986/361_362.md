### Synopsis

Terence has the Valley wondering why he's making frequent visits to Burrigan. Judy plans to do some match-making -- with herself when she sets her sights on the new Valley vet, Ben Green. Bob helps coach a young footballer with a promising future. Kelly arranges for a journalist friend from Sydney to come and write a review for the Club. CK mistakenly believes that Terence is going out with Kelly Shanahan.
