### Synopsis

Shirley refuses to move back home and accepts an offer to live with Maggie Sloan. A woman unable to get pregnant decides to find a surrogate mother. Donna takes a horse near to death from starvation under her care. The practical realities of using a surrogate mother cause nightmares for the people involved. Donna is left semi-conscious with only Jo to help her after she is kicked in the head by the horse she was helping.
