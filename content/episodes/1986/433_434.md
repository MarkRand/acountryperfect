### Synopsis

When Rowena declares that she is broke CK decides to quit school and join the work force. Peter becomes disillusioned when Alex pays no attention to his advances. Terence causes an argument with Rowena when he blames her for CK wanting to quit school. Alex and Peter help a man come to terms with being illiterate and proves it is never too late to learn. Matron Sloan devises a plan to show CK that being a member of the work force isn't as glamorous as he believes.
