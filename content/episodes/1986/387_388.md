### Synopsis

When a horse trainer is suspended for drugging her race horse, Kelly sets out to prove her innocence, Bob has a visit from an English solicitor who informs him that he has inherited an estate in England. A Vietnamese woman is shocked when the husband she thought was dead arrives in the Valley. A young boy disappears when he believes he is going to be taken away from his family. Kelly receives a threatening warning.
