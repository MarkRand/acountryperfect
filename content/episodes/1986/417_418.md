### Synopsis

Terence sees his son, CK, while in Sydney with Bethany, but the relationship is strained. Cookie bets Bob he can't go without a drink for one week. Alex takes charge of the clinic while Terence is away. Terence's devotion to Bethany has Shirley and Matron Sloan very concerned when he considers leaving the Valley to be with her permanently. Alex agrees to have a house-warming party under the agreement that Donna organises it.
