### Synopsis

A young boy with cerebral palsy has difficulty relating to his foster parents. Ben has chicken pox andis admitted to hospital, leaving Kelly to look after all of his animals. Bob and Cookie are descperately trying to gather botes to be elected onto the Rotex Committee. Judy enjoys her nursing duties when the patient under her care is Ben Green. The hospital staff are thrown into disarray every time the fire alarm goes off accidentally.
