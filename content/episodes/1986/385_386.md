### Synopsis

Judy advertises for a new flatmate and finds Mr Perfect. Kelly is assigned to do a behind-the-scenes story on the Burrigan Cup. A blind girl is told she will need a new guide dog but she refuses to give up her old dog. Cookie relies on Esme's dreams to pick the winner of the Cup. Terence and Ben help a blind girl adjust to her new dog. Kelly finds that her friends will not forget about the article she wrote on Dr. Wood as they continue to ignore her.
