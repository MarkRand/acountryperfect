### Synopsis

Tensions between Bob and Cookie reach boiling point so Cookie moves out. A woman with only six months to live decides to keep the truth from her husband. Kelly is thirlled when she receives an order for fifty litres of home-made ice cream. Cookie is furious when Bob crashes into Esme's taxi and refuses to pay for the damages. Shirley is delighted when she is accepted for Help Line.
