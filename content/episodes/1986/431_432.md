### Synopsis

Terence is lost for words when his ex-wife Rowena and son CK arrive at the clinic without warning. A drunken driver causes a car accident involving one of the hospital staff. Matron Sloan reprimands Donna for avoiding her nursing duties with one her the patients. CK tries devious means to bring Terence and Rowena together. Donna learns the hard way that when it comes to animals you sometimes have to be cruel to be kind. Shirley decides it's time to move back home.
