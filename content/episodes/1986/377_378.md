### Synopsis

Shirley has a nervous first night on Help Line when a caller is a ten-year-old boy afraid of being left alone. Bob plays nurse maid to Cookie when he is released from hospital. Sarah gives one of her pupils a book on sex education which lands her in trouble with the parents. Ben and Kelly are a bit worried when their new loo is going to look a bit outrageous. Bob plays a trick on Cookie to show him that he is not quite the fool Cookie thinks he is.
