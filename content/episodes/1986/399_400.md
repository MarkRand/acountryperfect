### Synopsis

Bob is angry at the lack of enthusiasm amongst the men of the Valley when he asks for volunteers to come forward and act as fire-fighters. Ben hears disturbing news about Kate and confronts her for an explanation. The bushflre threatens Wandin Valley. Frank fears for the safety of Ben and Kate who could both be trapped by the fire. The Valley pull together to save the Valley's existence.
