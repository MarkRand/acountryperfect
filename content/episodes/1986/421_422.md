### Synopsis

Donna sets out to save the Town Hall when she hears the council are going to build a shopping centre. Frank has to explain to Chloe what it means to lose a friend. Alex decides to stay in the Valley and become Terence's partner at the clinic. Bob, Esme, Donna and Jo put themselves in jail much to Frank's astonishment. Alex puts her medical opinions to one side when an old man explains why he should be left alone with his suffering. Peter tries to blackmail Alf Muldoon.
