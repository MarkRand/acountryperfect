### Synopsis

Kelly is surprised to hear that Judy believes that she and Ben are going out together. Muldoon thorws the hospital staff into turmoil when he alerts them of the impending arrival of the hospital inspectors. Simon gets depressed when he thinks that no one will miss him after he leaves the Valley. A simple boy runs away when he thinks his friends are making fun of him. Simon's friends gather to bid him farewell.
