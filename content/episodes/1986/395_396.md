### Synopsis

Shirley's concern for Frank grows when he refuses to talk about the shooting. Ben's relationship with the beautiful Kate Weilbourne blossoms - despite her father's obvious disapproval. Judy worries that the shooting has had a bad effect on Jo when she becomes very withdrawn. Alex Fraser becomes involved with a woman who doesn't realise she suffers with rheumatoid arthritis. Frank is unsure of his future in the police force when he has to go to Police Headquarters in Sydney for an enquiry on the shooting.
