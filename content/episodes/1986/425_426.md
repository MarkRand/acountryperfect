### Synopsis

Alex embarrasses Peter when she uses him as a medical guinea pig for nurses training. One of Peter's students threatens to jump off the top of a building. Shirley tries to find out what Muldoon is being so smug about. A young boy who feels he's an outcast from his school mates tries to kill himself Local man (and former Council toadie) Arnie Hodgekiss surprises the Valley locals when he takes a stand against Councillor Muldoon.
