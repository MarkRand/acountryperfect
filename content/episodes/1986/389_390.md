### Synopsis

Jo gets ajob with Bob Hatfield so she can help Judy save some money for the house she wants to buy. The local drunk of the Valley accuses an experienced nursing sister of mistreating the men under her care at a boarding house for homeless men. Kelly uncovers some interesting facts on the boarding house which prompt Terence and Frank to investigate. Bob and Jo help save an old horse's life.
