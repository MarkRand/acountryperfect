### Synopsis

A friend of Kelly's from the city comes to stay. Cookie and Kelly think up a fund-raising idea for the Club and decide on a darts match. Shirley asks Ben to hypnotise her, but it backfires and he hypnotises Esme instead! Matron Sloan and Shirley are set to challenge each other in the darts tournament. Kelly is worried about her friend when she appears to be giving up on life.>
