### Synopsis

Muldoon tries to blackmail Kelly into a partnership with her ice cream business. Esme, Bob, and Cookie go to a holiday resort which was recommended by Esme. Chloe is put into hospital for observation but the staff are worried when she seems far too withdrawn. A young man has to make a decision on whether he lets someone he loves live or die. Kelly's relationship with Muldoon worsens when Ben takes it upon herself to speak on her behalf. esme gets a shock when she reaches the resort -- it is far from what she expected.
