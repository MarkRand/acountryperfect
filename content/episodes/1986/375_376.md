### Synopsis

Bob thinks of a fund-raising idea to help Cookie with his money problems. A dairy farmer is persuaded that there is an easier way to make a living. It's Wandin Valley again Burrigan in the local dance competition, with Frank and Shirley desperate to win. Brendan is frustrated when Chloe reacts badly to his new friend, Sarah Anderson. It's touch and go to save a little boy's life when he almost drowns.
