### Synopsis

A young couple move into a house on a new estate and find they are in over their heads. Bob collapses after working at the housing estate but Alex is unable to determine the cause. There is tension between Alex and Matron Sloan as they battle against time to save Bob. Alex has to make a quick decision, one that Matron Sloan doesn't agree with. The locals battle against Muldoon to save a young couple's house.
