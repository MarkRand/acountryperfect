### Synopsis

An Aboriginal fruit picker, Joe Fisher, gets into a drunken scrap at the hotel, and Frank finds his son, Dennis, and a local boy, Robbie, playing truant. Fisher and his son meet the usual prejudices, which makes Joe reject his own background and culture. He is hospitalized after a drunken fall, and Terence is angered at the lack of sympathy displayed by some hospital staff. Tom, a local farmer, starts bulldozing a dam on his property, but Robbie and Dennis find an Aboriginal artefact on the site and Emma, an anthropologist friend of Molly's, takes action to protect it. She calls in National Parks and Wildlife who declare it an authentic site, and she persuades Joe to look at it. He explains the Aboriginal signs, recalls his childhood, and so the struggle in him between black culture and white man's drink is revived. Molly takes up the cause but offers Tom water from her own dam until he gets compensation for sitting his dam elsewhere. Joe moves on, carrying a crate of beer; but Dennis takes with him an armful of books about Aboriginal culture given him by Emma. [this description comes from the " ACP: Quality Soap " book]
