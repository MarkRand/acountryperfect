## Bloopers, Goofs & Continuity

**Mistakes and Oddities**
 
 
 
 
 
**With
any TV show or film, there is always the
possibility that minor mistakes will be made,
including discrepancies in time elapsed or
continuity gaps like a prop disappearing then
suddenly reappearing again.**

[**Discrepancies in
time**](#time-warps--timeline-inconsistencies)
**can occur for a
number of reasons. Generally 5 to 6 months
elapsed between when an episode was written and
when it actually went to air. This means that a
writer may have given a timeframe for events
based on the date on which he/she wrotethe
episode, rather than extrapolating and basing the
times on when the episode was expected to air.
He/she may also have just made a mistake (it
happens to the best of us). For the most part,
time and season on the show mirrored the time and
season in real world NSW at the time the show
aired. The few lapses that there are can become
frustrating, though, when you're trying to assign
timeframes.**

[**Continuity
gaps**](#continuity-errors)
**commonly occur
because scenes that we see in a certain order on
the screen are not necessarily shot in that
order. Parts of a scene may have to be reshot
afterwards and someone has to be responsible for
making sure that the positions of the props,
actors, clothing, mud, etc. are the same as in
the other shots. One little mistake and you might
have someone's jacket open in one shot, then
closed, then open again. Another continuity-type
gaff is when a character's personal details don't
remain constant (e.g. a sibling's name changes
from episode to another)**

[**Architectural
oddities**](architect.html)

**is
my catch-all phrase for those set characteristics
that don't seem to bear any resemblance to
reality, such as a wall outside a window when
there shouldn't be one. I've separated these
quirks from the continuity gaps because they may
not actually be mistakes (it may just be my
interpretation of building layouts that is
wrong).**

[**Other mistakes**](other.html)
**that can happen during the filming include having
the boom mike or crew members accidentally
appearing in a shot or having a "dead
body" move. In other words, things that
cause reality to intrude upon the fantasy world
that the show creators are trying to build.**

### Time Warps & Timeline Inconsistencies

**Wandin Valley Time (WVT)
Warps**
 
 
 
 
 
**"Wandin
Valley Time" is that magical time/space
phenomenon in which time on the show passes at a
fiercely accelerated pace to the "real
world", slows down drastically, or is just
plain wrong. Here are the ones that I've noticed:**

1. **Frank and Shirley
   were married in May 1982 (#**
   [**53/54**](/episodes/1982/53_54/)
   **).
   However, their first anniversary after
   Shirl's death came about in July of 1992
   (#931 - "**
   [**The
   Odds Against**](/episodes/1992/931_932/)
   **").**
2. **Also, their foiled
   celebrations in March 1991 (#809/810 -
   "**
   [**Doctors
   In Love**](/episodes/1991/809_810/)
   **")
   were supposedly for their 8th
   anniversary...it should have been their
   9th.**
3. **Darcy's sister Amber
   died in May 1992 (#919/920 - "**
   [**Sleeping
   Beauty**](/episodes/1992/919_920/)
   **")
   but was said to have died 18 months ago
   in an October 1992 episode (#955/956 -
   "**
   [**Falling
   Free**](/episodes/1992/955_956/)
   **")....that's
   about 13 months wide of the mark, which
   is a bit excessive.**
4. **In "**
   [**Wild
   Card**](/episodes/1993/995_996/)
   **"
   (ep #996) Tom's old friend Dave says that
   it's been 18 months since 17 May 1991,
   but the episode aired 20 April
   1993...which, if you're counting, is
   actually 23 months (5 months more than
   they say).**
5. **Terence's birthday
   in 1993 occurred in a June episode.
   However, in "**
   [**Every
   Move You Make**](/episodes/1993/943_944/)
   **"
   (#944), which aired in late August/early
   September 1992, his birthday was stated
   as being on Sept. 2.**
6. **In "**
   [**Crimes
   and Misdemeanors**](/episodes/1993/1023_1024/)
   **"
   (ep #1024 -- which aired on July 27,
   1993) Kate accuses Harry of
   "rabbiting on about Jules for the
   past 9 months". However, the**
   [**car
   accident**](/episodes/1993/1001_1002/)
   **( that paralyzed Jules) occurred happened
   in an episode which aired in May. There
   was an extreme case of WVT associated
   with this whole accident situation, in
   fact...2 months "apparently"
   passed from one episode to another when
   Harry was recovering in the hospital.**
7. **In "**
   [**Mozart
   Rules**](/episodes/1987/505_506/)
   **"
   (which aired in August 1987), Donna was
   preparing a Mozart's birthday party for
   Ben. However, Mozart was born January 27,
   1756, so August seems a bit wide of the
   mark.**
