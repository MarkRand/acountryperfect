## The Writers of A Country Practice

The enduring success and storytelling power of *A Country Practice* rested upon the talents of Australia's finest scriptwriters and story editors. Over twelve years and 1,058 episodes, the series balanced heartwarming rural community humour with groundbreaking, socially conscious drama.

> *"The creation of each episode was more a collaboration than a solitary accomplishment, and many talented writers, script editors, and storyliners had a hand in each episode beyond those credited on screen."*
