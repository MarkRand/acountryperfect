## Medical Cases & Staff Records

**Top Ten Causes of Death in
Wandin Valley**
 
 
 
 
 
**We're
still compiling this data but some of the
contenders for top cause of death in the Valley
are: car accidents, falls (from a variety of
surfaces), waterskiing, fires, cancer, heart
attacks, gun shot, hit and run, suicide, plane
crashes, drug overdose, AIDS, malpractice,
poisoning.
  
  
Please check back.**
 
 
 
 
**HOW
WILL WE DO IT? Our crack team of statisticians
will assign a numerical value to each death in
the Valley -- 3 points will be awarded if a
regular character dies that way, 2 points if a
guest character dies that way, and 1 point if the
mode of death is mentioned in passing but not
actually experienced over the course of an
episode (usually having to do with a relative of
one of the regular characters having died that
way).**
