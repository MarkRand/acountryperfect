## Social & Medical Issues

**Issues tackled on the show**
 
 
 
 
 
**"A
Country Practice" was well known for being
willing to bring tough issues to the viewing
public. The writers were very skilled at writing
stories that were well balanced, potent, and
thought provoking.**

**Issues
that were tackled on the show can be broken down
into two major groups -- medical issues,
including human and animal diseases and
conditions, and social issues, which included
such hard hitting topics as nuclear disarmament
and environmental disasters.**
