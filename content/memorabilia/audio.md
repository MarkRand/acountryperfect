## The Theme Music and Soundtrack Releases

The instantly recognisable *A Country Practice* theme was composed by celebrated Australian composer **Mike Perjanik**. Featuring a warm, melodic piano motif complemented by George Brodbeck's evocative harmonica solos, the tune remains one of the most enduring themes in Australian television history.

The theme was released as an official 7" vinyl single by EMI Custom Records (catalogue number EMI-836) and charted on the Australian national charts.

## All My Friends (1984 Vinyl LP)

In 1984, JNP Productions and Festival Records released a special charity and souvenir compilation album titled ***All My Friends*** (Catalogue No. L 38243). The record brought together the cast ensembles of Australia's two highest-rating television dramas at the time: *Sons and Daughters* and *A Country Practice*.
