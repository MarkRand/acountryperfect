## A Country Practice Books & Publications

Throughout its reign at the summit of Australian television ratings, *A Country Practice* spawned a celebrated collection of tie-in paperbacks, script adaptations, scholarly examinations, and glossy commemorative magazines.

### A Country Practice: Quality Soap

![Quality Soap Book Cover](/images/qualcover.jpg)

- **Authors:** John Tulloch and Albert Moran
- **Publisher:** Currency Press Pty Ltd (Paddington, NSW)
- **Published:** 1986
- **Format:** 303 pages, softcover, 13.9 cm x 21 cm
- **ISBN:** 0-86819-142-6

**Overview:**
A fascinating and seminal study delving into the factors that made *A Country Practice* such a ratings powerhouse and cultural phenomenon. The book examines the technical production process, script department workflows, casting decisions, contemporary media reviews, and the profound social resonance the series created with its audience. It also includes rare black-and-white production stills of the founding cast.

### Tales From Wandin Valley

![Tales From Wandin Valley Cover](/images/talescover.jpg)

- **Author:** Gay Scales
- **Publisher:** Penguin Books Australia Ltd (Ringwood, Victoria)
- **Published:** 1991
- **Format:** 315 pages, paperback, 11.2 cm x 18 cm
- **ISBN:** 0-14-014877-9

**Overview:**
Combining the dramatic story arcs from early key episodes into six complete novellas, Gay Scales affectionately retells some of the Valley's most cherished storylines, including the courtship and wedding of Frank Gilroy and Shirley Dean, the dramatic arrival and birth of Chloe Jones, and the romance between Dr. Simon Bowen and Dr. Vicky Dean.

### 10th Birthday Souvenir Magazine

![10th Birthday Souvenir Magazine Cover](/images/tenthcover.jpg)

- **Publisher:** Express Publications Pty Ltd (Lidcombe, NSW)
- **Published:** November 1991
- **Format:** 100 pages, glossy commemorative magazine
- **ISSN:** 1037-2377

**Overview:**
A lavish 100-page glossy collector's edition published in celebration of the show's 10th anniversary milestone on Channel 7. Packed with exclusive cast interviews, character retrospectives, behind-the-scenes photography, trivia, and a complete photographic record of a decade in Wandin Valley.

### Molly & Brendan: The Untold Story

![Molly and Brendan Cover](/images/books.jpg)

- **Author:** Suzanne Hawley
- **Publisher:** John Fairfax and Marketing
- **Published:** 1985

**Overview:**
A spin-off publication exploring the early backstory of Molly (Melissa Smithers) and Brendan Jones before their arrival at their hobby farm on Tallebung Road in Wandin Valley, detailing how they met in an Adelaide hospital and began their life together.
