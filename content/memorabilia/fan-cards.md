## Official Promotional Fan Cards

During the 1980s and 1990s, when *A Country Practice* consistently drew over 8 million weekly viewers, the Channel Seven network and JNP Productions produced official full-colour glossy postcards for fans who sent fan mail to the studio at Epping. These cards have become prized collector's items.
