## Home Video & DVD Releases

### The Complete Series on DVD (Umbrella Entertainment)

Following years of devoted campaigns by fans across Australia and the world, independent distributor **Umbrella Entertainment** licensed the entire archive of *A Country Practice* from JNP Productions.

Between 2006 and 2014, Umbrella released all **1,058 episodes** across chronologically ordered multi-disc box sets, making *A Country Practice* one of the few long-running drama series in world television history to have its complete run officially preserved and released on physical home video.

### Early VHS Releases

Prior to the DVD era, selected episodes and memorable telemovie-length specials were released on VHS in Australia and the United Kingdom, including:

- **The Pilot Episodes (Episodes 1 & 2)** - JNP Home Video
- **Molly's Farewell (Episodes 323 & 324)** - Commemorative video release of Anne Tenney's heart-wrenching departure
- **The 1000th Episode Celebration** - Seven Network Video
- **The Series Finale (Episodes 1057 & 1058)** - The complete two-hour Channel 7 conclusion featuring Terence and Rosemary's wedding
