## The 1994 Series (Network Ten)

Following the emotional 1993 finale on Channel Seven, Network Ten acquired the rights to *A Country Practice* and produced a revamped 30-episode continuation in 1994. Set in Wandin Valley with a mix of returning favourites (Esme Watson, Matron Maggie Sloan) and a largely new ensemble cast, the series aired for a single season.
