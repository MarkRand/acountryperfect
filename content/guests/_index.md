## Guest Stars on A Country Practice

*A Country Practice* served as a premier training ground and showcase for Australia's acting community. Over its 1,058 episodes, hundreds of established theatrical luminaries and emerging young talents made memorable guest appearances in Wandin Valley.

Many of Australia's internationally celebrated stars made early screen appearances on the show, including **Nicole Kidman**, **Russell Crowe**, **Delta Goodrem**, **Hugh Jackman**, **Naomi Watts**, **Simon Baker**, and **Toni Collette**.
