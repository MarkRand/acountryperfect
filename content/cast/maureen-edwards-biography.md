## Maureen Edwards Biography Biography

![](/photos/Cast/medwards1.jpg)

**Born:**
**April 17**
**in Napier,
Hawkes Bay (the "art deco" capital of New
Zealand)**

**Maureen initially trained as a
teacher. She became involved in amateur theatre and
eventually became the administrator of the Gateway
Players, a small theatrical group in Tauranga. She and
her husband Peter Tulloch also joined Christchurch's
Court Theatre and in 1977 she and Peter moved from New
Zealand to Australia with their three children (2 sons
and a daughter).**
