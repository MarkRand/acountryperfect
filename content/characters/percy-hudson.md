### Character Profile

**Percy Hudson**

**Perce Hudson was
an old farmer. His wife died sometime during the last
half of the 1980's. His daughter, Bernice, had taken off
with a young man, Sean Thomas, many years ago and was
living as a hippie for the past few decades. Perce never
really forgave her for not turning out better, especially
when she failed to turn up for his wife's funeral.**

**Totally against
Perce's wishes, his granddaughter,**
[**Darcy**](/characters/darcy-hudson/)
**, came to live with him in**
[**November 1991**](/episodes/1991/875_876/)
**and**
[**Bernice**](/characters/bernice-hudson/)
**showed up a few months
later, in**
[**April 1992**](/episodes/1992/911_912/)
**. He grew to enjoy having
them there, though he tried hard not to show it. In late
1992, he and**
[**Esme
Watson**](/characters/esme-watson/)
**began to cultivate a special friendship, though Esme was
careful to ensure that her name was never associated with
anything unseemly.**
