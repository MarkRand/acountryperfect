### Character Profile

**Matthew Herbert Tyler**

**Matt Tyler was the
son of Wagga Wagga vet Gil Tyler and his wife, Daphne.
Matt went out with Cathy Hayden a couple of time while
they attending New England University together (he was
pursuing an Arts degree). Matt quit Uni, however, to bum
his way through Europe and the South Pacific (including
surfing in Bali). His mother would send him money
whenever he needed it but when she died and his father
insisted there would be no more handouts, Matt had no
choice but to return to school. This time he decided to
take Veterniary Science at Sydney University, where he
passed with flying colours. He got a locum job in Bowral,
NSW, but a disastrous love affair forced him to flee to
Wandin Valley.**

**He stayed with
Cathy at her farm where he was constantly making a play
for her. When Cathy sold the farm, he stayed with the
Gilroys for awhile but begged Cathy to let him come live
with her in Boolaroo. He was a good vet, very kind
hearted, and this did not sit well with his father, whose
eye was always on the cash flow or lack thereof (since he
had been the one to initially finance both Matt's
education and the practice).**

**![Matt and Lucy's wedding](/photos/Char/matt_lucy_sm.jpg)**
**When Lucy Gardiner began living with
Cathy as well, she and Matt were initially just friends .
They fell hard for each other while Cathy was away in
Kakadu. Matt, who at the time was hosting a radio show
called "Valley Vet" on Burrigan Radio, proposed
to Lucy over the air. When Cathy sold Booloaroo, he and
Lucy lived separately with various residents of the
Valley before finally deciding to move in together. They
began renovating their dream home before they got married
but the house (and just about everything they owned) was
destroyed in a huge bush fire (that was set by a
pyromaniac). They eventually found and bought another
farmhouse (that is supposedly haunted by the ghost of a
little girl named Erin and boasts an old rickety wood
stove that Lucy calls "Ivan the Terrible").
