### Character Profile

**Kelly Shanahan**

**Kelly
had been in the restaurant business before coming to
Wandin Valley to write a cook book. When she arrived in
the Valley, she discovered that she only half owned the
house she had bought due to the shady dealings of her
accountant. She was forced to share the house with the
other owner, vet**
[**Ben
Green**](/characters/ben-green/)
**.
She took a job as hostess at the RSL club and worked as a
part-time reporter for the Burrigan Examiner, where her
views (and reviews) got her into hot water with the other
Valley residents.**

**She left Wandin
Valley in 1986 to become a TV reporter in Sydney. When
she left, she sold her half of the house to Ben.**
