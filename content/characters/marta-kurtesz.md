### Character Profile

**Marta Kurtesz**

**Marta was born in
Hungary. She came to Australia as a refugee at the age of
19 after her boyfriend Bela Szollos was killed in the
Budapest student riots. She began training as a nurse in
Sydney and worked in several large city hospitals before
her love of the outdoors and horses inspired her to move
out of the city.**

**![Matron Marta Kurtesz](/photos/Char/mksmbw.jpg)**
**She came to Wandin Valley to work
with old Dr. Clark and his younger partner,**
[**Dr. Terence Elliott**](/characters/terence-elliott/)
**. As the Matron of the
Wandin Valley Hospital, she found the pressures and
frustrations of dealing with supply shortages and
politics overwhelming at times and found relief in riding
her horse, the General. She had a brief relationship with
paraplegic Lachie Wilson which ended when the newly
confident Lachie returned to his former girlfriend and an
ill-fated affair with Ben Precott, the alcoholic manager
of the local pony club. Later on, she and Terence finally
got together and were engaged when she got a strange
letter from Sydney. It was from Bela. He hadn't died in
the riots after all and had flown into Australia
illegally to take her back with him. She left with him in**
[**April
1983**](/episodes/1983/125_126/)
**.**
