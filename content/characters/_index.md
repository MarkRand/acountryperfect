## Characters of Wandin Valley

The heart of *A Country Practice* was its rich cast of characters residing in the fictional rural township of Wandin Valley, New South Wales. From the dedicated staff at the Bush Nursing Hospital to the colourful locals who frequented the Wandin Valley Club and the Gilroy home, here are the profiles of the series' most memorable characters.
